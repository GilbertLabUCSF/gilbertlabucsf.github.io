Research

Epigenome Engineering

CRISPRi, CRISPRa, and CRISPRoff for programmable control of gene expression without cutting DNA.

Functional Genomics

Genome-scale screens to map genetic interactions and identify synthetic lethal targets in cancer.

Drug Resistance

Modeling therapy response in prostate cancer, AML, glioblastoma, and lung cancer.

Combinatorial Perturbations

Engineered Cas12a for multiplexed chromatin editing and higher-order genetic interactions.
